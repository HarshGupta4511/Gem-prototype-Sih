# GeM Procurement Guidelines (Demo Reference)

> Demo document for the SIH26100 prototype knowledge base. Summarises public GeM procurement
> concepts for retrieval-augmented explanation. Not legal advice.

## 1. Bid evaluation on GeM
- Bids are evaluated against the tender's stated eligibility and technical criteria only.
- Evaluation must be transparent, criterion-wise, and documented with reasons.
- Financial bids of technically non-compliant bidders are not opened.

## 2. Essential eligibility documents
- Valid GST registration and PAN are mandatory for most goods/services tenders.
- Udyam (MSME) registration is required to claim MSE purchase preference / EMD exemption.
- Bidders must not be blacklisted or debarred by any government entity during the debarment period.

## 3. Make in India (MII) preference
- Class-I local supplier: local content ≥ 50%. Class-II: ≥ 20%.
- Purchase preference is given per the prevailing Public Procurement (Preference to Make in India) Order.
- Local content declarations should be supported by a certificate or self-declaration as the tender requires.

## 4. Clarifications
- Buyers may seek clarifications on ambiguous or missing documents within a stated deadline.
- Clarifications cannot be used to submit altogether new credentials that did not exist at bid deadline.

## 5. Decision authority
- The buyer's authorised procurement officer records the final qualification decision.
- System-generated evaluations are decision *support*; they do not replace officer judgement.
