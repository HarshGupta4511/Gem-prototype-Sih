# Make in India — Local Content Guidelines (Demo Reference)

> Demo document for the SIH26100 prototype knowledge base.

## 1. Local content classes
- **Class-I local supplier**: local content equal to or above **50%**.
- **Class-II local supplier**: local content equal to or above **20%** but below 50%.
- **Non-local supplier**: local content below 20%.

## 2. How local content is assessed
- Local content % = (value added in India / total value) × 100, excluding taxes and duties as applicable.
- Bidders submit a self-declaration or auditor/CA certificate stating the local content percentage,
  depending on the tender's value threshold and conditions.

## 3. Verification expectations
- The declaration should name the manufacturing location(s) in India.
- Inconsistency between the declared percentage and supporting cost break-ups is a review trigger:
  the officer should seek clarification rather than auto-failing the bid.

## 4. Tender-specific thresholds
- Individual tenders may set a minimum local content (e.g. ≥ 50% for Class-I preference).
- This platform evaluates the tender's own threshold deterministically; this document provides
  the policy context shown alongside the result.
