## Tender: Procurement of Industrial Pump Systems
- Tender No: CPCL-DEMO-2026-001. Estimated value: ₹4.5 crore.

## Eligibility (summary)
1. Bidder must hold valid GST registration (active status).
2. Bidder must hold a valid PAN.
3. Udyam/MSME registration must be active where MSE benefits are claimed.
4. Minimum average annual turnover of **₹10 crore** over the last three financial years.
5. Minimum **5 years** of experience in supply of industrial pumps or similar rotating equipment.
6. **OEM authorization** is mandatory for non-OEM bidders.
7. **Make in India**: minimum **50%** local content (Class-I).
8. Valid EPFO and ESIC registrations.
9. Income Tax compliance: latest ITR filed.
10. Bidder must not be blacklisted/debarred by CPCL or any government body.
11. Complete bid documents: PAN certificate, GST certificate, Udyam certificate at minimum.

## Evaluation notes
- Each condition maps to a deterministic rule in the platform (see tender requirements).
- Missing mandatory documents may trigger a clarification request at the officer's discretion.
- Final qualification is decided by the authorised Procurement Officer.
