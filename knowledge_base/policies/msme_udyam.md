# MSME / Udyam Registration — Reference (Demo)

> Demo document for the SIH26100 prototype knowledge base.

## 1. Udyam registration
- Udyam is the government registration for Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises.
- Format: `UDYAM-<STATE>-<DISTRICT>-<NUMBER>`, e.g. `UDYAM-MH-19-0012345`.

## 2. Enterprise categories (indicative)
- **Micro**: investment up to ₹1 crore, turnover up to ₹5 crore.
- **Small**: investment up to ₹10 crore, turnover up to ₹50 crore.
- **Medium**: investment up to ₹50 crore, turnover up to ₹250 crore.

## 3. Validity and verification
- Registration should be **active** on the bid due date; expired or cancelled registrations
  do not support MSE benefits.
- Name on the Udyam certificate should match the bidder's legal name (allowing for
  standard abbreviations like Pvt./Private/Ltd./Limited).

## 4. Benefits in procurement
- MSEs may claim EMD exemption and purchase preference where the tender allows.
- The tender's own conditions decide whether Udyam is mandatory or preferential —
  this platform reads that from the tender requirements, not from this document.
