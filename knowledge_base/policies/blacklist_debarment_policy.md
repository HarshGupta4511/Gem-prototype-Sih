# Blacklisting and Debarment Policy — Reference (Demo)

> Demo document for the SIH26100 prototype knowledge base.

## 1. Principle
- A bidder blacklisted or debarred by CPCL, the Ministry, or any government authority is
  ineligible to participate during the debarment period.

## 2. Risk treatment
- A confirmed blacklist/debarment hit is a **critical risk signal**. It overrides an otherwise
  high compliance score: compliance score and risk level are independent by design.
- The platform marks the integrity requirement as FAIL and risk as CRITICAL, but the final
  decision (including considering appeals or expired orders) rests with the Procurement Officer.

## 3. Evidence required
- Debarment record should cite: authority, order number, period, and reason.
- Name matching should consider aliases and former names; fuzzy matches below the identity
  threshold are flagged for officer review rather than treated as confirmed hits.

## 4. Officer review
- If the bidder claims the order was stayed or has expired, the officer records an override
  with supporting documents. The original system finding is always preserved.
