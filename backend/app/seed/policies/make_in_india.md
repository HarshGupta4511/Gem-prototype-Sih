# Make in India — Local Content Guidelines (Demo Reference)

> DEMO DOCUMENT — fictional content for prototype demonstration.

## 1. Objective
The demo Make in India preference policy promotes procurement of goods with
domestic value addition. This document is fictional and for prototype use only.

## 2. Local content threshold
- For this demo tender, bidders must declare local content of **at least 50%**.
- Local content = (ex-factory price − imported content) / ex-factory price × 100.

## 3. Classes of suppliers
- Class-I local supplier: local content ≥ 50%.
- Class-II local supplier: local content ≥ 20% but < 50%.
- Non-local supplier: local content < 20%.

## 4. Declaration
- Every bidder submits a self-declaration of local content percentage.
- The declaration must state the place(s) where value addition occurred.

## 5. Verification
- Declarations may be cross-checked against OEM authorization letters and
  manufacturing location certificates.
- False declarations are grounds for debarment under the demo debarment policy.

## 6. Evaluation
- A bid below the 50% threshold fails the local-content requirement.
- Where extraction of the declared percentage is uncertain, the requirement is
  marked REVIEW_REQUIRED for officer verification.

## 7. Exemptions
- No exemptions apply to this demo tender; all bidders are evaluated equally.

*End of demo document.*
