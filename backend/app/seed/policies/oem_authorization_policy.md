# OEM Authorization Policy — Demo Reference

> DEMO DOCUMENT — fictional content for prototype demonstration.

## 1. Purpose
This fictional policy sets the norms for Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM)
authorization letters in the demo procurement platform.

## 2. When authorization is required
- Bidders offering equipment manufactured by another company must submit an
  OEM authorization letter from the manufacturer.
- Direct manufacturers are exempt but should declare manufacturing status.

## 3. Contents of the letter
- Name of the OEM (the Original Equipment Manufacturer).
- Name of the authorized bidder.
- Confirmation that the authorization is valid ("Yes" / valid) on the bid date.
- A validity date ("Valid Until") for the authorization.

## 4. Validity
- Letters without a future validity date are treated as expired.
- Authorization from a manufacturer whose own credentials are in doubt is
  flagged for review.

## 5. Verification
- The demo platform records the OEM name and authorization status from the
  submitted letter.
- Cross-checks against mock manufacturer records may be performed.

## 6. Evaluation
- Where the tender requires OEM authorization, a missing letter is a MISSING
  finding on that requirement.
- Ambiguous authorization statements are marked REVIEW_REQUIRED.

## 7. Fraud
- Fabricated authorization letters are grounds for debarment.

*End of demo document.*
