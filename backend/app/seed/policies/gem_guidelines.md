# GeM Procurement Guidelines — Demo Reference

> DEMO DOCUMENT — fictional content for prototype demonstration.

## 1. Purpose
These demo guidelines summarise how procurement on the Government e-Marketplace
(GeM) is expected to work for this prototype. They are fictional and carry no
legal force.

## 2. Registration
- Sellers must hold a valid GSTIN and PAN before listing goods or services.
- Udyam-registered micro, small and medium enterprises receive preference
  markings on their catalogue listings.

## 3. Bid evaluation principles
- Evaluation is criterion-based: each requirement carries a published weight.
- Price bids are opened only after technical qualification is established.
- Any bidder may seek clarification on published terms before the bid deadline.

## 4. Statutory compliance (§4.2 of the demo GTC)
- A bid without a valid GST registration is non-responsive.
- PAN details must match the Income Tax Department records for the bidder.

## 5. Documentation
- Certificates must be current on the bid due date; expired documents are
  treated as non-compliant.
- Where a portal check is unavailable, the case is marked for officer review
  rather than auto-passed or auto-failed.

## 6. Debarment
- Bidders debarred by any government buyer are ineligible for the debarment
  period stated in the order.

## 7. Officer authority
- The procurement officer may override a system finding with recorded reasons.
- All overrides are preserved in the audit trail with the original finding.

*End of demo document.*
