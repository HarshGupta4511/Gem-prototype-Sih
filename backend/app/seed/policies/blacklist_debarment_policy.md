# Blacklisting and Debarment Policy — Demo Reference

> DEMO DOCUMENT — fictional content for prototype demonstration.

## 1. Purpose
This fictional policy describes blacklisting and debarment handling in the
demo platform. It has no legal standing.

## 2. Definitions
- **Blacklisted**: barred from all government procurement for a stated period.
- **Debarred**: barred by a specific buyer or for specific tender categories.

## 3. Grounds for debarment (demo)
- Submission of fraudulent experience or turnover certificates.
- False Make in India / local content declarations.
- Bribery, collusion, or bid rigging.
- Repeated failure to honour awarded contracts.

## 4. Effect on evaluation
- A blacklist/debarment hit is a critical risk signal.
- The bid is marked NOT_RECOMMENDED; the officer still takes the final call.
- The listing authority, reason and period are shown as evidence.

## 5. Verification
- The demo platform queries a mock blacklist with the bidder's legal name.
- A clean result is recorded as "verified clean" with the mock label.

## 6. Appeals
- In a real process the bidder could appeal; the demo records officer comments
  only.

## 7. Data hygiene
- Mock blacklist records in this prototype are entirely fictional companies.

*End of demo document.*
