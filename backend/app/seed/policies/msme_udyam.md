# MSME / Udyam Registration — Demo Reference

> DEMO DOCUMENT — fictional content for prototype demonstration.

## 1. Scope
This fictional note describes Udyam registration categories used by the demo
verification platform. It is not an official publication.

## 2. Enterprise categories
- **Micro**: investment up to ₹1 crore and turnover up to ₹5 crore.
- **Small**: investment up to ₹10 crore and turnover up to ₹50 crore.
- **Medium**: investment up to ₹50 crore and turnover up to ₹250 crore.

## 3. Registration
- Registration is via the Udyam portal and yields a number of the form
  UDYAM-XX-00-0000000.
- The certificate must be valid (not expired, not cancelled) on the bid date.

## 4. Benefits in demo tenders
- MSE bidders may be considered under relaxed norms where the tender allows.
- Purchase preference may apply as per the tender's stated conditions.

## 5. Verification
- The demo platform checks the Udyam number against mock Udyam records.
- An EXPIRED registration yields an EXPIRED finding, never an automatic pass.
- If the portal is unreachable, the finding is REVIEW_REQUIRED.

## 6. Name consistency
- The enterprise name on the certificate must match the bidder's legal name;
  mismatches are flagged for officer review.

## 7. Renewal
- Demo certificates carry a validity date; bidders must renew before expiry.
